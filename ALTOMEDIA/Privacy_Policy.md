# DIVO777 — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 August 2026_

**DIVO777** ("DIVO777", "the App", "we", "us", "our") is a free-to-play mobile slot-machine
game developed and published by **ALTOMEDIA** ("ALTOMEDIA", "the Developer"). The App is
distributed through the Google Play Store and is intended for installation and use on
Android devices that meet the minimum Android version requirement (Android 6.0 / API
level 23 or higher).

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the App may collect,
how that information is used, how long it is kept, the choices you have, and your rights
under applicable data-protection laws. By installing and using DIVO777 you confirm that
you have read and understood this policy and you agree to the practices described here.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please do not install or
continue using the App. You may uninstall the App at any time through the application
management screen of your Android device.

---

## 1. The type of game and the meaning of "credits"

DIVO777 is a **single-player entertainment application** styled after a classic Las
Vegas slot machine. It is designed to provide a fun, casual spinning experience with
animated reels, themed symbols, an Auto Spin feature, an on-screen pay table and a pay
lines reference panel.

The App uses a virtual in-game currency called **credits**. Credits have **no monetary
value**, they cannot be purchased with real money, they cannot be converted into real
money, they cannot be withdrawn, they cannot be transferred to other players, and they
cannot be exchanged for any goods or services outside the App. The credit balance is
stored **locally on your device** and exists only to let you play. If you run out of
credits, the App will offer free replenishment options so that you can keep playing.

Because no real money, real gambling, or prizes of monetary value are involved,
DIVO777 is **not a gambling product**. It is a **free social/casual game** and it does
not facilitate, enable, or encourage real-money gambling of any kind.

## 3. Information the App uses locally

In order to function as a game, DIVO777 stores a small amount of state **on your
device only**. This data is never sent to ALTOMEDIA and never leaves the device under
normal operation:

- **Credit balance and game statistics** — the number of credits you currently hold,
  your last bet, the last Auto Spin setting you used, and aggregate statistics such as
  total spins performed. This is stored in the App's private storage area so that when
  you reopen the App it remembers where you left off.
- **Sound and music preference** — whether you have muted or unmuted the in-game audio.
  The App remembers this choice between sessions.
- **User interface preference** — whether you last opened the pay table or pay lines
  panel, so the App can present a consistent experience.

This local data is **not personal data** in the sense of most privacy regulations
because it is not linked to your identity and it never leaves the device. It is only
used to provide the game experience you requested. If you uninstall DIVO777, all of
this local data is removed along with the App.

## 4. Technical information that may be processed by the operating system

Like every Android application, DIVO777 necessarily interacts with the Android
operating system and, when network calls are made, with network infrastructure. Some
technical data may be processed by the operating system or by network carriers as part
of providing the service. This includes:

- **Network availability** — the App checks whether the device has an active network
  connection. This check is performed locally through the Android `ConnectivityManager`
  API and does not transmit personal data.
- **Standard network transport information** — when the App communicates over the
  network (for example, to fetch optional configuration), the carrier or network
  provider may process routing metadata such as IP address and connection timing.
  This is inherent to how the internet works and is handled by your provider under
  its own privacy policy, not by the App.
- **Crash and stability information** — the App relies on the standard Android
  runtime. If the App crashes, the Android operating system may generate a crash log.
  Depending on your device settings, this log may be sent to Google through the
  standard Android system reporting mechanism. ALTOMEDIA does not run its own crash
  reporting SDK inside the App.

We do not use this technical information to identify you personally.

## 5. Children and minors

DIVO777 is intended for a **general audience**. The App does not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Because the App does not require account creation
and does not collect personal data from any user — adult or minor — it does not
perform any processing that would require parental consent under laws such as COPPA
(Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or the equivalent provisions in your
jurisdiction.

Although the App is styled after a slot machine, it involves **no real-money play**
and **no possibility of winning anything of monetary value**. Nevertheless, we
encourage parents and guardians to discuss responsible device use with minors, and to
use the parental controls available on Android (including Google Play's parental
controls and the Google Play Family Library features) to decide which applications are
appropriate for their family.

If you believe a minor has provided information to us through the App in a way that
should not have happened, please contact us using the details in Section 9 and we will
take appropriate action.

## 6. Your rights and choices

Because DIVO777 does not collect personal data through the App itself, most of the
rights granted by data-protection laws (such as access, rectification, erasure, and
portability) can be exercised by you **directly on your device**:

- **Right to access** — you can view the local game state (credit balance, settings) at
  any time within the App itself.
- **Right to erasure** — uninstalling the App deletes all local data associated with
  it. No separate request is needed.
- **Right to object** — because there is no profiling and no behavioral advertising,
  there is nothing to object to. If you do not wish to use the App, simply uninstall it.
- **Right to withdraw consent** — the App does not rely on consent as a basis for
  processing because it does not collect personal data requiring consent.

If you have any question about how the App works or about this policy, you can contact
us at the address given in Section 9.

## 7. Data retention

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted by the App, there is no remote
retention to manage. Local game state is retained on your device for as long as the
App is installed, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the App.
