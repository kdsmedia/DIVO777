B. FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
-----------------------------------------
DIVO777 brings the timeless charm of the classic Las Vegas slot machine to
your Android device. Spin five glowing reels packed with fruit, bells, bars,
sevens, and lucky gems, watch the pay lines light up when you hit a winning
combination, and let Auto Spin keep the reels rolling while you sit back and
enjoy the show.

DIVO777 is a FREE-TO-PLAY entertainment game. There is no real money, no
wagers, and no prizes of monetary value. Every credit in the game is a virtual
in-game currency that exists only to let you play. If you run out, the game
offers free replenishment options so the fun never has to stop.

GAME FEATURES

• Classic five-reel slot machine
  DIVO777 captures the look and feel of a vintage fruit machine: spinning
  reels, satisfying reel-stopping sounds, and a payout table you can study at
  any time.

• Ten themed symbols
  From cherries and lemons to bars, bells, and sevens, each symbol has its
  own payout value. Open the in-game pay table to see exactly what every
  combination is worth.

• Multiple pay lines
  Several pay lines crisscross the reels, giving you many ways to land a
  winning combination. Tap the pay lines button to see every line the game
  recognises, so you always know where the action is happening.

• Auto Spin
  Prefer to sit back and watch? Activate Auto Spin and the reels will keep
  turning automatically. You stay in control: stop Auto Spin at any time with
  a single tap.

• Built-in pay table and pay lines reference
  New to slot machines? The integrated pay table explains every symbol and
  every payout. The pay lines panel shows you the shape of each line so you
  can learn the patterns at your own pace.

• Local, private, and free
  Your credit balance and game settings are stored locally on your device.
  No account is required, and the game does not ask you for personal
  information.

WHO IS DIVO777 FOR?

DIVO777 is for anyone who enjoys a colourful, casual spinning experience. It
is suitable for a general audience and contains no real-money play, no

HOW TO PLAY

1. Open the app — you start with a generous balance of free credits.
2. Choose your bet per spin using the bet controls.
3. Tap SPIN to set the reels in motion.
4. Watch the reels stop and the pay lines light up on any win.
5. Use the pay table and pay lines buttons to learn the symbol values and
   the line shapes.
6. Want to keep your hands free? Switch on Auto Spin and let the reels roll.
7. Out of credits? The game offers free replenishment so the fun continues.

real-money gambling be a licensed, real-gambling app with specific
country-level restrictions. DIVO777 does NOT fall into that category because
it involves no real money and no prizes of monetary value. It should be
listed under the Casino category as a free social/casual game. Make the
non-gambling nature of the app explicit in the store description (the copy
above does this) and keep the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service publicly
accessible.
